Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what data the BitaPro app (construction site log for Android and Windows) uses, where it is stored and how you control it. In short: your data is yours. BitaPro has no servers of its own and does not sell data.

1. Where your data is stored

  • On your device. Everything you record (projects, site log, photos, signatures, quantities, drawings, payroll, finances, etc.) is stored on the phone or PC where you create it. On Android the database is encrypted.
  • In your own cloud, if you turn it on. If you connect your Google Drive or OneDrive account to sync or share, the data is stored in your account, in an app folder. BitaPro keeps no copy.
  • End-to-end encryption. Before leaving your device, everything is encrypted (AES-256) with a key that only your devices and the people you invite have. Neither BitaPro, Google nor Microsoft can read the content. The key is protected by your recovery password and your 24-word code; if you lose them, nobody can recover the encrypted data.

2. Google and Microsoft accounts

Only if you choose to connect them, BitaPro requests these permissions, and uses them solely to store and sync your projects:

PermissionPurpose
drive.file (Google)Create and read only the files and folders the app itself creates (or that you open with it). It cannot see the rest of your Drive.
drive.appdata (Google)A private, hidden app folder in your Drive that holds your encrypted keychain.
Files.ReadWrite.All (Microsoft)Read and write the project files in your OneDrive and those another person shares with you.
Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder (Microsoft)The app's private folder in your OneDrive (your encrypted keychain).
User.Read, offline_access (Microsoft)Know which email you signed in with and keep the session open to sync.

Sign-in data (tokens) is kept in your device's secure storage and is never sent to BitaPro.

Use of Google user data

BitaPro's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. That data is used only to provide the sync feature you see in the app; it is not transferred to third parties, not used for advertising, and not read by any person.

3. Sharing with other people

Only a project's owner can invite other people, by email, with a link and a PIN. The owner decides which modules each person can view or edit, and can remove access at any time: from then on the project key changes and that person can no longer read anything new. Shared data stays in the owner's cloud; permissions are enforced by Google or Microsoft.

4. AI assistant (ObraBot)

ObraBot is optional and only works with your own key for the AI service (Google Gemini). When you use it, the text of your question and the project data the assistant needs to answer are sent directly from your device to that service, with your key, and are subject to its terms. BitaPro receives nothing. If you do not set a key, nothing is sent.

5. Error reports

To fix bugs, the app may send technical error reports (Sentry) without personal data: they do not include your name, email, device name or the content of your projects. You can turn them off in Settings → "Anonymous error reports".

6. Advertising

The app may show advertising at any time, but never based on your data or information: your projects, photos, location or anything you record are not used to choose or personalize ads. If an ad provider is added, non-personalized ads will be used and it will be named here.

7. Device permissions

PermissionPurpose

CameraTake photos, make camera measurements and scan an invitation QR code.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Your data stays as long as you keep it. The change history of a shared project is kept in the owner's cloud.
  • Deleted items stay in the trash for 30 days and are then removed.
  • To delete everything: delete the project ("Delete for everyone" if you are the owner), disconnect the account and delete the "BitacoraObra" folder from your Drive or OneDrive; uninstalling the app deletes the data on the device.
  • You can revoke BitaPro's access from your Google or Microsoft account security settings.

9. Children

BitaPro is a professional tool for civil works and is not directed at children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may change at any time. When it does, you will be notified in the app and the new version will be published here with its date.
