The construction site log, on your phone and PC

BitaPro brings the daily site log, quantities, drawings and resources together in one app for Android and Windows. Your data is yours: it stays on your device and, if you choose, encrypted in your own Google Drive or OneDrive account.

Daily log

Weather, staff, equipment, activities, GPS photos, visitors and a folio number for each day. PDF, Word and Excel reports.

Quantity takeoff

Excel-like spreadsheets with formulas, camera measurements and links to project items.

Drawings

PDF, image and DXF viewer with distance and area measurement, tabs and split screen.

Resources and finances

Inventory, tools, PPE, fuel, payroll and payment certificates.

Schedule and map

Schedule, S-curve and a map with progress by section.

Teamwork

Sync your devices and share a project by module: each person sees or edits only what the owner allows.

No servers of our own. BitaPro does not store your projects on any server of ours. Sync uses your own Google Drive or OneDrive account and everything is end-to-end encrypted: neither BitaPro, Google nor Microsoft can read your data.

Platforms

Android and Windows. Works offline; syncing happens when there is internet.
